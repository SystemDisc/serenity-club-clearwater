Project context and design references

The project includes exterior siding and trim, substrate and weather protection, soffit and selective fascia work, preparation and painting. Additional repairs and trades are addressed as needed. $100,000 is a fundraising goal, not a fixed total project price.

G02 draws on established fundraising layouts: a place-led photograph, a clear appeal, a visible goal, a concise scope, and a distinct response area. No sample campaign artwork was copied.
