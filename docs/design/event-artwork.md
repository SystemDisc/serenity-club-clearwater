# Event illustrations

Created September 28, 2026 with OpenAI image generation. These are intentionally illustrated community-poster graphics, not photographs of the Club or its members. Optimized to 1200px WebP.

Common event titles select coordinated artwork; an event’s optional external image URL overrides it. Uploaded originals remain available via the flyer link. Other events use their uploaded image, or the coffee illustration when no image exists.

## community

Asset: src/serenity/artwork/community.webp

Prompt: Create a finished landscape 3:2 editorial illustration for the Serenity Club of Clearwater community events website. It MUST be unmistakably a flat, playful hand-drawn illustration, like a friendly screen-printed community poster. Bold simplified shapes, slightly imperfect ink outlines, restrained gouache brush marks, warm cream paper ground, forest green, burnt orange, muted sage and little pink accents. Objects drawn with whimsical simplified proportions, no realistic shading or realistic lighting. Absolutely NO photography, photorealism, 3D rendering, realistic interiors, people, logos, letters, numbers, or text. No frames, no collage of multiple panels. Composition fills the landscape naturally with ample breathing room and a few loose doodle rays, stars or autumn leaves. Charming and welcoming, not corporate stock art, not a mock photograph. A lively arrangement of three illustrated ceramic coffee mugs in coral, sage and cream, overlapping drawn circular ripples suggesting conversation and connection, with a small orange sun, a sprig of leaves, friendly hand-drawn steam curls and star doodles. Pale warm butter background. No recognizable real venue.
